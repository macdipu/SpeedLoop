WHAT'S INSIDE

Eight tools, one dashboard.

Everything you'd normally need three separate apps for — built to work together, and to work offline.

Live speedometer

Current, average, and top speed, plus heading and altitude, updated live from GPS.

HUD mode

A mirrored display for windshield reflection — a clean, glanceable heads-up view for night driving.

Trip recording

Start a trip and SpeedLoop logs your route in the background, screen off included.

Trip analysis & map

Review distance, duration, and top/average speed, with the full route on an interactive map.

Loop dashcam

Speed, GPS, and timestamp burned into the video. Oldest clips auto-delete — incidents are kept safe.

Speed limit alerts

Set a limit and get a spoken warning plus an on-screen alert the moment you go over.

GPX export

Export any trip as a standard .gpx file, or import existing tracks to view inside SpeedLoop.

Made to fit you

Units, theme, and language, all customizable — SpeedLoop adapts to how you drive.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN

Nothing to sign up for.
Nothing sent to us.

  • No backend, no account. Trips and clips live in local storage on your device only.
  • No ads or analytics SDKs. The app doesn't know you're using it — we don't either.
  • You control sharing. GPX export and clip sharing only happen when you tap Export or Share.
  • Delete anytime. Remove a single trip, or clear all app data in one tap.

GETTING STARTED

Up and running in under a minute.

1

Grant location once

SpeedLoop asks for location the first time you open the speedometer or start a trip — nothing is tracked before that.

2

Start a trip or record

Tap Start Trip to log your route, or open Dashcam to begin loop recording with the live overlay.

3

Review or export

Browse trip history on the map, export to GPX, or share a dashcam clip straight from the app.
