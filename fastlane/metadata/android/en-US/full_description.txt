SpeedLoop turns your phone into a GPS speedometer, trip recorder, and dashcam — no account, no ads, no data sent to any server but the map.

REAL-TIME SPEEDOMETER
See your current, average, and top speed at a glance, plus heading and altitude, updated live from GPS. Switch to HUD mode to mirror the display on your windshield for a heads-up view at night.

TRIP RECORDING & ANALYSIS
Tap Start Trip and SpeedLoop logs your route in the background, even with the screen off, so you get a complete record when you're done: distance, duration, average and top speed, and the full route on an interactive map.

LOOP-RECORDING DASHCAM
Record dashcam video with your live speed, GPS location, and timestamp burned into the footage. Loop recording automatically deletes your oldest clips when storage fills up, so you never have to manage space manually. Clips are flagged and kept safe when you mark an incident.

SPEED LIMIT ALERTS
Set a speed limit and get a spoken warning the moment you go over it, plus an on-screen alert — no need to glance down at your phone.

GPX EXPORT & IMPORT
Export any trip as a standard .gpx file to use in other mapping and GIS tools, or import GPX tracks to view them inside SpeedLoop.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Every trip, route, and video clip is stored only on your device. SpeedLoop has no login, no cloud sync, no ad network, and no analytics SDK — the only network requests it makes are for OpenStreetMap map tiles, so the map can be drawn.

Built for drivers, riders, and anyone who wants an accurate offline speedometer and a dashcam that doesn't need a subscription.

PERMISSIONS USED
• Location — for speed tracking, trip routes, and the live map
• Camera & microphone — for dashcam recording, only while you're recording
• Notifications — to show the persistent "trip recording" status required by Android while tracking runs in the background
